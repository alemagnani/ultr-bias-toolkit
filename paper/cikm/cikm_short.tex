\title{Harmonic Weighting is Count-Only Inverse-Variance Weighting:
A Rigorous Asymptotic Analysis of Intervention Harvesting on Heavy-Tailed Click Logs}

\author{Anonymous}
\affiliation{\institution{Anonymous}\country{}}
\renewcommand{\shortauthors}{Anonymous}

\begin{document}

\begin{abstract}
Intervention-harvesting estimators of position bias~\cite{agarwal2019estimating} combine
per-cell click-rate estimates across many $(q,d,\mathrm{position})$ cells with widely
varying impression counts. We show that the \emph{harmonic} weighting scheme
$\omega_i=N_k^i N_{k'}^i/(N_k^i+N_{k'}^i)$ is exactly the count-only inverse-variance
optimum---the classical generalized-least-squares solution of
Aitken~\cite{aitken1934least}---and that the appropriate finite-sample diagnostic on real
click logs is the \emph{plug-in delta-method asymptotic variance}
$\widetilde V(\omega)$ of the self-normalized weighted ratio
estimator~\cite{owen2013mc}, not bootstrap. We replace bootstrap analyses with
$\widetilde V$ on the full KDD Cup 2012 Track 2 log
(235M impressions; 4.6M interventional pairs at positions $(1,2)$) and find an
\textbf{asymptotic relative efficiency} of harmonic over uniform of $4.5\times$,
rising to $\sim 5\times$ on high-information cells. Plug-in $\widetilde V$ agrees with
empirical click-resample standard error within 1\%. Cochran's $Q$ heterogeneity
test restricted to high-information cells rejects the Position-Based Model
at $z\approx 253\sigma$, isolating model misspecification from estimator efficiency.
Synthetic and Yahoo-LTR semi-synthetic experiments with ground truth corroborate
the efficiency claim.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Click-based position-bias estimation via intervention
harvesting~\cite{agarwal2019estimating,wang2018position} combines per-cell estimates of
the form $\hat\theta_k^i=C_k^i/N_k^i$ across many $(q,d)$ cells. On real sponsored-search
logs, impression counts $N_k^i$ are extraordinarily heavy-tailed: on the full
KDD Cup 2012 Track 2 release (235\,M impressions, 4.6\,M interventional pairs at
adjacent positions), \textbf{60.7\% of pairs have $N_1+N_2\le 5$ while 0.1\% of
pairs carry 42\% of all impressions.} Equal-weighting such heteroscedastic
estimators is statistically suboptimal by a century of classical theory:
Aitken's generalised-least-squares theorem~\cite{aitken1934least} mandates
inverse-variance weights, which the meta-analysis
literature~\cite{hedges1985meta,borenstein2009meta} has championed for fifty years.

Recent ULTR work proposed \emph{harmonic weighting}
$\omega_i=N_k^i N_{k'}^i/(N_k^i+N_{k'}^i)$ as a variance-reduced replacement for
the uniform and min-count schemes of~\cite{agarwal2019estimating}. Existing empirical
validation has relied on \emph{bootstrap} variance estimates on real click logs,
but on heavy-tailed data the bootstrap is the \emph{wrong} instrument for
inverse-variance-style weights (Section~\ref{sec:method}, point~3).

\textbf{This paper makes three contributions.}
\textbf{(1)} We identify harmonic weighting as the count-only inverse-variance
optimum for the intervention-harvesting estimator, anchoring it in classical
GLS / meta-analysis theory (Section~\ref{sec:method}).
\textbf{(2)} We replace bootstrap-based variance assessment with the
\emph{plug-in delta-method asymptotic variance}
$\widetilde V(\omega)$ of the self-normalised weighted ratio
estimator~\cite{owen2013mc,vdv1998asymptotic}, and report
\emph{asymptotic relative efficiency} (ARE) as the headline metric
(Section~\ref{sec:plugin}).
\textbf{(3)} On full KDD we measure ARE(harmonic, uniform) $=4.5$ (rising to
$\sim 5$ on the high-information quartile), with plug-in agreement to within
1\% of empirical click-resample std and a Cochran-$Q$ test at $z\approx 253\sigma$
that decisively rejects PBM on the data---separating estimator efficiency from
model fit (Section~\ref{sec:kdd}).

\section{Setup and Method}
\label{sec:method}

\paragraph{Estimator.} Under the Position-Based Model (PBM),
$P(\mathrm{click}\mid q,d,k)=p_k\, r_{q,d}$. Given an interventional set
$I_{k,k'}$ of $(q,d)$ pairs that appeared at both positions $k$ and $k'$, the
ratio of position-bias parameters is estimated as
\begin{equation}
\hat R_{k,k'}=\frac{\sum_i \omega_i\, \hat\theta_k^i}{\sum_i \omega_i\, \hat\theta_{k'}^i},
\quad \hat\theta_k^i=\frac{C_k^i}{N_k^i}.
\label{eq:Rhat}
\end{equation}
$\hat R$ is a self-normalised weighted ratio estimator. Any count-only weight
$\omega_i=\omega(N_k^i,N_{k'}^i)$ preserves ratio-unbiasedness under
PBM~\cite{agarwal2019estimating}.

\paragraph{Harmonic as count-only IVW.}
For Bernoulli observations,
$\mathrm{Var}(\hat\theta_k^i)=\theta_k^i(1-\theta_k^i)/N_k^i$. Aitken's
theorem~\cite{aitken1934least} states that the minimum-variance unbiased linear
combination assigns weight $\omega_i\propto 1/\mathrm{Var}(\hat\theta_i)$. With
$\theta$ treated as constant across cells (i.e.\ the count-only regime), the IVW
optimum for the pair $(k,k')$ is the \emph{harmonic mean} of the two cell counts.

\begin{theorem}[Count-only IVW characterisation]
\label{thm:harmonic_ivw}
Among all weights of the form $\omega_i=\omega(N_k^i,N_{k'}^i)$, the harmonic
weight $\omega_i=N_k^i N_{k'}^i/(N_k^i+N_{k'}^i)$ minimises the count-only
variance surrogate $V_k(\omega)+V_{k'}(\omega)$, with
$V_k(\omega)=\sum_i \omega_i^2/N_k^i / (\sum_i \omega_i)^2$.
\end{theorem}

\paragraph{Plug-in asymptotic variance.} Let
$A=\sum_i\omega_i\theta_k^i$ and $B=\sum_i\omega_i\theta_{k'}^i$. By the delta
method~\cite{vdv1998asymptotic} and conditional independence of clicks at
different positions,
\begin{equation}
\widetilde V(\omega)=\frac{\sum_i\omega_i^2\,\theta_k^i(1-\theta_k^i)/N_k^i}{A^2}
+\frac{\sum_i\omega_i^2\,\theta_{k'}^i(1-\theta_{k'}^i)/N_{k'}^i}{B^2},
\label{eq:Vtilde}
\end{equation}
and $\mathrm{SE}(\hat R)=|\hat R|\sqrt{\widetilde V(\omega)}$. Plugging in
$\hat\theta_i$ (with light shrinkage toward the marginal) gives a deterministic
functional of the observed counts---the textbook self-normalised IS variance
estimator~\cite{owen2013mc,kong1992ess,swaminathan2015snips}.

\paragraph{Why bootstrap fails here.} Three reasons.
(i)~Uniform weighting averages over $\sim$60\% near-singleton pairs, producing a
smooth-looking bootstrap distribution whose tightness reflects averaging-of-noise,
not estimator precision.
(ii)~Pair-resampling perturbs the high-information cells that drive
inverse-variance estimators, penalising any concentrated weight scheme by
construction.
(iii)~Bootstrap cannot disentangle estimator efficiency from PBM
misspecification, but $\widetilde V$ does (a model-internal efficiency comparison
is well-defined even when PBM is rejected).

\section{Plug-in ARE and Calibration}
\label{sec:plugin}

\paragraph{Asymptotic Relative Efficiency.}
For two schemes $\omega_a,\omega_b$, $\mathrm{ARE}(a,b)=\widetilde V(\omega_b)/\widetilde V(\omega_a)$
quantifies the sample-size multiplier scheme~$b$ pays to match scheme~$a$:
$\mathrm{ARE}=c$ means $b$ needs $c\times$ the data of $a$ for equal precision.
$\widetilde V$ scales as $1/\mathrm{ESS}(\omega)$ with
$\mathrm{ESS}(\omega)=(\sum_i\omega_i)^2/\sum_i\omega_i^2$ (Kong's effective
sample size~\cite{kong1992ess}).

\paragraph{Calibration check.} On a subsample of KDD ($f=0.001$), plug-in SE
matches empirical click-resample SE within 1\% for both uniform (0.1306 vs.\
0.1321) and harmonic (0.1113 vs.\ 0.1120). The plug-in is correct on this data.

\paragraph{Cochran's $Q$ for PBM heterogeneity.}
On the same data, per-cell log-ratios $\log(\hat\theta_k^i/\hat\theta_{k'}^i)$
have IVW-pooled variance $\sigma_i^2$; the heterogeneity statistic
$Q=\sum_i w_i(\log\hat\rho_i-\log\hat R)^2$ with $w_i=1/\sigma_i^2$ is
$\chi^2_{n-1}$ under PBM. Restricting to cells with $\ge 5$ clicks at each
position (high-information cells) yields a clean PBM-violation test.

\section{Experiments on Real Click Logs (KDD Cup 2012)}
\label{sec:kdd}

\paragraph{Data.} Full KDD Cup 2012 Track 2 release: 149.6\,M raw rows,
235.6\,M effective impressions, 63.8\,M unique $(q,\mathrm{ad},\mathrm{pos})$
cells, 8.2\,M clicks (CTR 3.49\%). Pair $(1,2)$ interventional set:
\textbf{4.61\,M $(q,\mathrm{ad})$ pairs}, $132.3$\,M impressions. Median pair
size $4$; max $2{,}466{,}174$. Of these, 60.7\% have $N_1+N_2\le 5$, while
0.1\% of pairs hold 42\% of all impressions.

\paragraph{Headline (Table~\ref{tab:are_full}).}
$\mathrm{ARE}(\text{harmonic},\text{uniform})=4.53$ on full KDD.
\textbf{Uniform needs $\sim 4.5\times$ the impressions to match harmonic's
precision}, with $\mathrm{SE}_U/\mathrm{SE}_H\approx 2.13$. The min-count
heuristic of~\cite{agarwal2019estimating} achieves ARE$=4.41$; the count-only
oracle reaches $4.57$. The full-$\theta$ oracle, which would be optimal under
Aitken's theorem if $\theta_i$ were known, blows up because plug-in
$\hat\theta_i$ is itself too noisy on the singleton-dominated tail.

\paragraph{Stratified ARE (Table~\ref{tab:strat}).} Harmonic dominates uniform
$5\times$ on the highest-impression quartile and is essentially tied on the
noise quartiles. The efficiency gain comes from \emph{not} averaging singletons
on equal footing with high-information cells.

\paragraph{PBM is decisively rejected.} On the 16,132 high-information pairs
(both positions $\ge 5$ clicks at $f=1.0$),
$Q=6.17\times 10^4$, $\mathrm{df}=16{,}131$, $Q/\mathrm{df}=3.83$,
$z\approx (61700-16131)/\sqrt{2\cdot 16131}\approx 253\sigma$. This holds across
scales ($z\approx 50$ at $f=0.1$; $z\approx 7.6$ at $f=0.01$). PBM rejection
explains why schemes converge to slightly different $\hat R$ on KDD (uniform
1.68, harmonic 1.71, $\theta$-oracle 2.34): Theorem~1
ratio-unbiasedness~\cite{agarwal2019estimating} holds \emph{under PBM}, which is
violated. The ARE result is unaffected---it is a model-internal efficiency
comparison.

\paragraph{Synthetic and Yahoo-LTR.}
On synthetic data with PBM by construction and ground-truth $p_k$, harmonic
reaches a target precision $\varepsilon$ with $1/1.3$--$1/1.94$ the impressions
that uniform needs (median 1.31$\times$, max 1.94$\times$ across imbalance
regimes; figure deferred to appendix).
On the semi-synthetic Yahoo LTR dataset, harmonic reduces MSE versus uniform by
up to $55\%$, consistent with ARE $\approx 2$. Both confirm the ARE prediction
in regimes where ground truth exists.

\section{Conclusion}

Framing harmonic weighting as the count-only inverse-variance optimum
(Aitken~\cite{aitken1934least}) and replacing bootstrap-based variance
assessment with plug-in delta-method $\widetilde V$ (Owen
\S9.2~\cite{owen2013mc}) yields a rigorous, calibration-validated efficiency
comparison on heavy-tailed real click logs. On KDD Cup 2012 Track 2,
harmonic delivers $\mathrm{ARE}=4.5$ over uniform, with PBM
heterogeneity quantified separately by Cochran-$Q$ at $z\approx 253\sigma$.
Future work: shrinkage variants of the full-$\theta$ oracle for cells where
$\hat\theta_i$ is well-estimated; richer click models~\cite{wang2018position,yue2010beyond}
where PBM is known to fail.
